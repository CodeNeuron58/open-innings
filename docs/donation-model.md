# Funding

Open Innings is AGPL-3.0 and free forever. Every scoring rule, every career
stat, every export and every live link is available to everyone, with no
feature paywall and no plan to add one.

This file explains how it pays for itself, where that is going, and the one
promise it deliberately refuses to make.

---

## The principle

**The scorer is the labour; the viewers are the audience.**

Scoring a three-hour match takes concentration. The person doing it is the
reason the data exists at all, and they are the last person who should be
charged for it — or interrupted by an ad under their thumb while they record a
ball.

So:

1. **No feature is ever gated.** Not one. Paying changes nothing about what the
   app can do.
2. **No ad ever appears on a scoring screen.** Ads run on scorecards and share
   screens, where people are reading rather than tapping.
3. **The match creator sees no ads on their own match**, anywhere.
4. **Self-hosting is free and ad-free.** There is no ad server in your own
   build, and nothing phones home.

---

## Today: ads, plus an optional subscription

Servers cost real money — database, bandwidth, and rendering a share card for
every match. Two things pay for that:

- **Banner ads on viewer surfaces.** Public scorecards and share screens, for
  people who did not score the match.
- **A supporter subscription**, ₹49 a month or ₹199 a year, which removes ads
  everywhere on every device signed into that account.

The subscription unlocks nothing else, because there is nothing else to unlock.
The moment ₹199 buys a feature, "every feature free for everyone" stops being
true, and that sentence is worth more than the revenue.

### Why there is no lifetime plan

This is the one place the project refuses to do what would obviously sell
better, so the reasoning is worth stating.

**Never sell a term longer than you can confidently serve.** The servers
currently run on a student credit with a known expiry. A lifetime plan is an
unbounded promise against a bounded runway.

Worse: if the app ever does go fully free, everyone who bought "ads off
forever" paid for something that became free. That reads as a bait-and-switch
even when it isn't one.

Monthly and yearly both sit inside the horizon that can be promised. Lifetime
doesn't. That is the whole test.

**And the lifetime plan already exists, for free.** _What if you disappear,
what if the price rises, what if I stop wanting to pay_ — the answer to all
three is the licence. It is AGPL-3.0. Any club can run its own copy, ad-free,
forever, paying nothing. That is a more trustworthy answer than a purchase, and
it is true.

---

## Where this is going: community-funded

The destination is the Lichess model — **ads removed entirely, running on
donations.** Advertising is how a project with no users bootstraps, not what it
should look like at rest.

The trigger is simple and will be stated publicly when it happens: when
recurring donations cover hosting and maintenance, the ad units come out.

Until then the number that matters is **supporters and clubs, not revenue**. A
sponsor is convinced by "1,200 clubs, 400 supporters", not by a small amount of
money from fifty people. Prices are set low for the same reason — ₹199 for a
year is not a decision anyone agonises over, and more supporters is worth more
here than more per supporter.

---

## What money is spent on

- Hosting, database, storage, SSL and the domain
- Maintainer time — this is not a hobby-hours project, and pretending otherwise
  is how open-source maintenance dies
- Design, accessibility work and security review
- Bounties for contributions that matter

**Not** on: paid marketing to buy vanity metrics, speculative assets, or
anything that would compromise a user's privacy or sell their data.

---

## What we will never do

- ❌ Ads during live scoring — ever
- ❌ Ads for the person who scored the match
- ❌ A "pro" tier that locks features that used to be free
- ❌ Selling user data. What is collected and why is at
  [openinnings.com/privacy](https://openinnings.com/privacy)
- ❌ Corporate lock-in — the codebase stays AGPL-3.0
- ❌ "Contact us for enterprise pricing"
